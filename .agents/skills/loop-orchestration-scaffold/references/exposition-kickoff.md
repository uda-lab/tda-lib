# Exposition kickoff

Runs once per exposition request, before any drafting: from an explicit writing request. Its output is the agreed brief in the note
Issue (`references/note-issue.md`); nothing else is recorded.

```markdown
Read the request, source context and authorised issue decisions, including
relevant comments, before asking questions. Clarify what the readers need
to learn or decide, what they already know, and what content or depth is
excluded. Ask only about consequential unresolved choices. Record the
agreed brief in the exposition issue, distinguishing assumptions and open
questions; carry later explicit corrections into drafting and review.
```

Resolve from reliable existing context before asking: language and register, actual length
and proof depth, the notation source, authorship, source availability, and the delivery route
and authority. Authorship and bibliographic facts come from the sources (`bib-integrity`,
`sot-integrity` when present) — a handle does not establish authorship, and a directly
verifiable fact is not an owner question. Source availability and provisional status follow
the loop skill’s source-fidelity and release policy.

Separate the readers' mathematical background, their familiarity with the method (for
example, no Lean), and what they asked to learn (for example, what formalisation found). An
unfamiliar method constrains *how* the findings are explained; it does not make the requested
findings irrelevant.

The brief is natural language. No questionnaire transcript, no numerical section ratios, no
status file. Ask the remaining consequential questions once, on the note Issue (the
notification rule applies: no alert for them), and continue with everything that does not
depend on the answers. Later explicit corrections amend the brief in the Issue body; drafting
and review follow the amended brief.
