# Realizations of simple graphs

Consulted manifestations, attribution and rights: [ReebOrdering-aa7d10f](references.md#ReebOrdering-aa7d10f), [mathlib-0df444a](references.md#mathlib-0df444a).

## The realization

For `V : Type u` and `G : SimpleGraph V` the cells are

```lean
Cells G = V ⊕ Σ _ : G.Dart, unitInterval
```

one point for each vertex and one copy of the unit interval for each dart, that is, for each
oriented edge. The vertex summand carries the discrete topology `⊥`, whatever topology `V` may
have, and the edge summand the sigma (disjoint-union) topology of copies of the unit interval;
each vertex cell is open (`isOpen_vertexCell`). The relation `CellRel G` has exactly two
generators,

```lean
CellRel.symm d t  : (d, t) ~ (d.symm, σ t)
CellRel.start d   : (d, 0) ~ d.fst
```

orientation reversal and attachment of the start of a dart. `Realization G := Quot (CellRel G)`
carries the quotient topology, with quotient map `quotMk`. Two cells have the same image exactly
when the equivalence relation generated by `CellRel G` relates them (`quotMk_eq_quotMk_iff`, with
`Relation.EqvGen`). The realization is neither given the discrete topology nor replaced by a
quotient by components.

`vertex G v` and `edgePoint G d t` are the images of the cells. Reversal gives
`edgePoint G d.symm (σ t) = edgePoint G d t` (`edgePoint_symm`); the start equation is
`edgePoint G d 0 = vertex G d.fst` (`edgePoint_zero`), and the end equation
`edgePoint G d 1 = vertex G d.snd` (`edgePoint_one`) follows from the two. Every point is a vertex
or an edge point (`ind`), so functions and continuous maps out of the realization are determined
by their values on vertices and edge points (`function_ext`, `hom_ext`). `function_ext` is not
named `funext`, so opening the namespace does not shadow `_root_.funext`.

No finiteness, local finiteness, nonemptiness, decidable adjacency, separation or topology of the
vertex type is assumed anywhere in the construction, the universal property, the maps or the
component equivalence.

## Universal property

Vertex data `fv : V → Y` and edge data `fe : G.Dart → unitInterval → Y` with

```lean
hsymm  : ∀ d t, fe d.symm (σ t) = fe d t
hstart : ∀ d, fe d 0 = fv d.fst
```

descend to `lift G fv fe hsymm hstart : Realization G → Y`, with `lift_vertex` and
`lift_edgePoint` by `rfl`. The lift is unique among all functions with these values
(`lift_unique`). It is continuous as soon as every `fe d` is (`continuous_lift`): continuity is
checked through the quotient map, on the discrete vertex summand and on each interval cell
separately; no topology on `V` is involved. For continuous edge data
`fe : G.Dart → C(unitInterval, Y)`, `continuousLift : C(Realization G, Y)` is the bundled form,
with its evaluation lemmas, uniqueness among continuous maps (`continuousLift_unique`) and
`existsUnique_continuousLift`.

Distinct vertices stay distinct (`vertex_injective`). The proof is set-level: the lift to
`Option V` sending a vertex to itself, an endpoint of an edge to its vertex and an interior edge
point to `none` respects both generators. No continuous retraction to the vertices is asserted.
The realization is empty for an empty vertex type and nonempty for a nonempty one
(`instIsEmptyRealization`, `instNonemptyRealization`).

## Paths

`edgePath G d : Path (vertex G d.fst) (vertex G d.snd)` is the edge map on `[0, 1]`
(`edgePath_apply`), and `walkPath G p` concatenates the edge paths of a walk `p`
(`walkPath_nil`, `walkPath_cons`). Hence reachable vertices have joined images
(`joined_vertex_of_reachable`). Every edge point is joined to the first endpoint of its dart
(`joined_edgePoint_vertex`), by the straight path from `t` to `0` in the unit interval followed
by the edge map.

## Maps induced by graph homomorphisms

For `V : Type u`, `W : Type v` and a graph homomorphism `φ : G →g H`,

```lean
realizationMap φ : C(Realization G, Realization H)
realizationMap φ (vertex G v)      = vertex H (φ v)
realizationMap φ (edgePoint G d t) = edgePoint H (φ.mapDart d) t
```

both formulas by `rfl`. It is the bundled lift of these data; the two compatibilities hold because
`φ.mapDart` commutes with reversal and with the first endpoint. Identity and composition hold as
equalities of continuous maps (`realizationMap_id`, `realizationMap_comp`), with three
independent vertex universes for composition. A graph homomorphism preserves adjacency, so every
edge goes to an edge and no edge is collapsed to a vertex. Nonadjacent vertices and distinct edges
may be identified.

## Path components

`componentLabel G : Realization G → G.ConnectedComponent` sends `vertex G v` to the component of
`v` and `edgePoint G d t` to the component of `d.fst`; the two generators are respected because
adjacent vertices lie in the same component. With the discrete topology chosen locally on the
component type the label is continuous, so it is constant along paths
(`componentLabel_eq_of_joined`, through `TDA.Reeb.joined_eq_of_discrete`). Hence two vertices
have joined images exactly when they are reachable (`joined_vertex_iff`), and every edge point
lies in the path component of a vertex (`mk_edgePoint`).

```lean
componentsEquiv G : ZerothHomotopy (Realization G) ≃ G.ConnectedComponent
componentsEquiv G (ZerothHomotopy.mk (vertex G v))         = G.connectedComponentMk v
componentsEquiv G (ZerothHomotopy.mk (edgePoint G d t))    = G.connectedComponentMk d.fst
(componentsEquiv G).symm (G.connectedComponentMk v)        = ZerothHomotopy.mk (vertex G v)
```

The forward map is the label on path components, and the inverse sends a graph component to the
class of a representing vertex, well defined because walks give paths. All three formulas hold by
`rfl`, and both inverse laws are fields of the equivalence: the vertex case is `rfl` and the edge
case is `mk_edgePoint`.

**Naturality.** For every `φ : G →g H` and every path component `c`,

```lean
componentsEquiv H (TDA.Reeb.componentMap (realizationMap φ) c) = (componentsEquiv G c).map φ
```

(`componentsEquiv_componentMap`), where `TDA.Reeb.componentMap` is the library's map on
`ZerothHomotopy` induced by a continuous map (see `reeb-components.md`) and the right-hand map is
mathlib's `SimpleGraph.ConnectedComponent.map`. Both generator cases hold by `rfl`. The same
square is available as an equation of functions (`componentsEquiv_comp_componentMap`) and in the
inverse direction (`componentMap_componentsEquiv_symm`). The identity and composition laws of
`componentMap` give `componentMap (realizationMap Hom.id) = id`
(`componentMap_realizationMap_id`) and the composition law across three universes
(`componentMap_realizationMap_comp`). No category of graphs is built.

**Connectedness.** `connected_iff_pathConnectedSpace : G.Connected ↔ PathConnectedSpace
(Realization G)`. Both sides include nonemptiness: mathlib's `Connected` carries
`Nonempty V`, and `PathConnectedSpace` is equivalent to a nonempty subsingleton `ZerothHomotopy`
(`pathConnectedSpace_iff_zerothHomotopy`). The proof transports both conditions along
`componentsEquiv`; an empty graph is neither connected nor has a path-connected realization. The
statement concerns path components, not connected components.

## Names and universes

The construction lives in namespace `TDA.Reeb.GraphRealization` (`TDA/Reeb/GraphRealization.lean`)
and the fixtures in `TDA.Reeb.Examples.GraphRealization` (`TDA/Reeb/GraphRealizationExamples.lean`);
no production module imports the fixtures. The vertex type has an arbitrary universe `u`, and
`Cells G` and `Realization G` live in `Type u`. `realizationMap`, its laws, the naturality square
and the corollaries allow independent universes for the vertex types of source, target and, for
composition, a third graph; the same-universe case is an instance. The graph is an explicit
argument of `vertex`, `edgePoint`, `lift`, `continuousLift`, `componentsEquiv` and the naturality
square `componentsEquiv_componentMap`; `realizationMap φ` infers it from `φ`. The only private
declaration is the straight path in the unit interval behind `joined_edgePoint_vertex`.

Fully qualified names are distinct from all other library names, but some short names are not.
`Realization`, `instTopologicalSpaceRealization`, `quotMk`, `quotMk_surjective`,
`isQuotientMap_quotMk`, `continuous_quotMk`, `lift`, `lift_unique` and `hom_ext` also occur in
`TDA.Reeb.CylinderPresentation` (`reeb-constructible-space.md`), `lift`, `lift_unique`, `hom_ext`
and `ind` in `TDA.Reeb.ReebQuotient` (`reeb-quotient.md`), and `edgePoint` and
`continuous_edgePoint` in `TDA.Reeb.RGraph` (`reeb-rgraph.md`). A file opening more than one of
these namespaces qualifies the overlapping names. Among the fixtures, `fold`, `half`,
`pointGraph`, `swapHom`, `swap_naturality` and `swap_ne_id` also occur in other fixture
namespaces.

## Fixtures

`TDA.Reeb.GraphRealizationExamples` proves:

- **Empty vertex type.** For every `[IsEmpty V]` in any universe, the realization, its path
  components and the graph components are empty, and both connectedness statements are false
  (`empty_realization_isEmpty`, `empty_zerothHomotopy_isEmpty`,
  `empty_connectedComponent_isEmpty`, `empty_not_connected`, `empty_not_pathConnectedSpace`).
- **One isolated vertex.** The realization of `⊥ : SimpleGraph Unit` is a point: it is `Unique`
  and homeomorphic to `Unit` (`pointUnique`, `pointHomeomorph`). Both component types have exactly
  one element (`pointComponentsUnique`, `point_card_connectedComponent`,
  `point_card_zerothHomotopy`), and the graph is connected with path-connected realization
  (`point_connected`, `point_pathConnectedSpace`).
- **One edge on two vertices.** For `⊤ : SimpleGraph Bool` and the dart `d₀ : false → true`,
  `edgeHomeomorph : Realization ⊤ ≃ₜ unitInterval` sends `vertex false ↦ 0`, `vertex true ↦ 1`,
  `edgePoint d₀ t ↦ t` and `edgePoint d₀.symm t ↦ σ t`, with inverse `edgePoint d₀`. The reversed
  dart at the reversed parameter is the same point (`edge_reversed`), and distinct parameters,
  interior ones included, give distinct points (`edge_param_injective`, `edge_interior_ne`).
- **Two disjoint edges.** On `Bool × Bool` with edges `(a, false) — (a, true)`, the path
  components are equivalent to `Bool` (`twoEdgesComponents`, `twoEdges_card`), the vertex classes
  `c₀` and `c₁` are distinct (`c₀_ne_c₁`), and every class is one of them
  (`twoEdges_classification`).
- **A two-edge path folded onto one edge.** `fold : pathGraph 3 →g ⊤` sends `0, 1, 2` to
  `false, true, false`. It identifies the nonadjacent ends `0` and `2` (`fold_identifies_ends`)
  and, pointwise off the shared vertex, the distinct edges `0 — 1` and `1 — 2`
  (`fold_identifies_edges`, separated by a set-level height). It is a graph homomorphism, not an
  edge contraction. The generator formulas hold on all three vertices and on the darts `0 → 1`,
  `2 → 1` (image `d₀`) and `1 → 2` (image `d₀.symm`, with reversed edge coordinate
  `fold_coord_d12`). Naturality is evaluated at the interior point `1/2` of both orientations of
  the second edge (`fold_naturality_d21`, `fold_naturality_d12`).
- **A permutation of two components.** `swapHom` exchanges the two disjoint edges. Its induced map
  on path components sends `c₀ ↦ c₁` and `c₁ ↦ c₀` and is not the identity (`swap_c₀`, `swap_c₁`,
  `swap_ne_id`). At `c₀` both sides of the naturality square are the class of `(true, false)`,
  which is not the class of `c₀` (`swap_naturality`).
- **Infinite edgeless graphs.** In an edgeless graph on any type, distinct vertices lie in
  distinct path components (`bot_class_injective`). On `ℕ` the vertex map and the class map are
  injective, there are infinitely many path components and the realization is not path connected
  (`nat_vertex_injective`, `nat_class_injective`, `nat_zerothHomotopy_infinite`,
  `nat_not_pathConnectedSpace`): no finiteness is assumed. On `ℝ` the realization is not path
  connected although `ℝ` is (`real_class_injective`, `real_not_pathConnectedSpace`): the topology
  of the vertex type is not used.

The fold lands in a connected graph, whose component type is a subsingleton, so its naturality
square holds whatever the component maps are and does not discriminate at the level of
components. Its discriminating content is in the generator and orientation formulas, which
distinguish the two darts of the target edge. The component-level negative control is the swap:
both of its classes are distinct, the induced map is not the identity, and the square at `c₀` has
a value different from `c₀`'s own class.

**de Silva, Munch and Patel.** *Categorified Reeb Graphs*, Notation 2.7, describes a finite
ℝ-graph as the quotient of the disjoint union of vertex points `Vᵢ × {aᵢ}` and edge intervals
`Eᵢ × [aᵢ, aᵢ₊₁]` by the endpoint identifications. This is context for the cell construction: there
the vertex and edge sets are finite, edges are oriented by height and there is no reversal
relation. The realization of arbitrary simple graphs, its path-component equivalence and its
naturality are library constructions, not results of the paper. The library's realization of
height-indexed presentations, `TDA.Reeb.CylinderPresentation.Realization`, is a different
construction (`reeb-constructible-space.md`).

**Mathlib and TauCeti.** Pinned mathlib has the affine simplicial complex of a simple graph,
`Geometry.SimplicialComplex.ofSimpleGraph` in `V →₀ 𝕜`
(`Mathlib/Analysis/Convex/SimplicialComplex/AffineIndependentUnion.lean`, for `[DecidableEq V]`,
`[DecidableEq 𝕜]` and a linearly ordered field `𝕜`), with the vertices as 0-simplices at basis
vectors and the edges as 1-simplices; it has no quotient description, lift, induced maps or
path-component API. No file under `Topology` or `AlgebraicTopology` uses `SimpleGraph`. The
realization `SSet.toTop` is that of simplicial sets. `TopCat.zerothHomotopyEquiv` is not a
realization construction: it compares `ZerothHomotopy X` with the connected components `π₀` of the
singular simplicial set of `X`. TauCeti, at revision `3b4a078d372e27f93b0c181e6b67df93797d1a02`, has
a realization of abstract simplicial complexes under
`TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialComplex/Realization/`. Its `SimpleGraph` files lie under
`TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/` and in
`TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/FiniteType/SimpleGraph.lean`, and no path under
`TauCeti/Topology/` or `TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/` names `SimpleGraph`. This is a partial check,
not a content-level inspection of every file, so it does not establish that TauCeti has no
simple-graph realization. Nothing from TauCeti is used, and TauCeti is not a dependency.
