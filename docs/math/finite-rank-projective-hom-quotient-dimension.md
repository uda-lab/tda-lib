# Finite-grid Hom-module and Hom-cokernel dimensions

Let `k` be any field and `S` a finite bounded lattice. The interval-pair poset is

`P = { (a,b) ∈ WithTop S × WithTop S | a ≤ b }`,

ordered coordinatewise, where `WithTop S` adjoins a genuinely new top element. Let `A` be its
actual incidence algebra. Let `e` be the sum of the vertex idempotents `δ_(p,p)` indexed by
diagonal pair vertices `p = (a,a)`, where `a ∈ WithTop S`. Let `I` be the two-sided ideal
generated by `e` (equivalently, by these diagonal-pair vertex idempotents). Write `C = (A/I)ᵐᵒᵖ`.
The finite hook generator `L = HookSum` has opposite
endomorphism algebra `T = (End L)ᵐᵒᵖ`, which is identified with `C` by the existing hook-
endomorphism algebra equivalence. The objects of `ModuleCat T` and `ModuleCat C` are left
modules, encoding the actual right-module convention.

For `p ∈ P`, the minimal complement consists of the minimal elements of
`{a ∈ P | ¬a ≤ p}`. The number `D(P) = maxComplementCard (S := P)` is the maximum cardinality of
these minimal complements over all `p ∈ P`. The classification and grid counts are given in
[minimal-complement cardinalities in finite grids](finite-grid-complement-cardinality.md).

For a persistence module `M`, let `H(M) = homFunctor.obj M` be the actual natural-transformation
module from `L`, and let `K(M)` be the actual categorical cokernel of `H` applied to the
two-injective differential of `M`. If `M` is pointwise finite-dimensional, the library proves
that the canonical restricted-field dimension of `K(M)` is finite and
`HasProjectiveDimensionLE K(M) (D(P))`. The bound uses the actual quotient-module action through
the algebra equivalence and the existing diagonal-quotient projective-dimension theorem. The
scalar restriction and its comparison with the natural Hom-space action are constructed with
explicit identity-on-vectors linear equivalences; neither compatible scalar actions nor a
projective-dimension estimate is an input.

For an actual coordinate grid `G : CoordinateGrid n` with nonempty factors and `n ≥ 1`, a
pointwise finite-dimensional `M : PModule k G.Point` satisfies
`HasProjectiveDimensionLE K(M) (2*n)` and
`HasProjectiveDimensionLE (H(M)) (2*n-2)`. Every supplied certified minimal finite-rank-projective
resolution of `M` has zero `termAt j` for `j > 2*n-2`. This is a highest-nonzero-degree statement,
so adding terminal zero terms does not affect it; no bound on the stored `length` field is claimed.
The bounds hold over arbitrary fields and do not require a finite coefficient field or a
finite-dimensionality premise beyond pointwise finite-dimensionality of `M`.

These results are the ordinary ambient projective dimensions of the actual modules. Their
real-coordinate minimal-resolution transfer is proved separately in
[the finite-presentation resolution result](finite-rank-projective-resolution.md#the-real-coordinate-degree-bound),
using the actual finite-grid Kan reconstruction and zero-padded uniqueness. Together they prove
the `2*n-2` upper degree bound for every certified minimal resolution of an originally finitely
presented real-coordinate module. They do not prove a lower witness, equality, or the final
signed-stability result, and they do not claim a sharp bound for arbitrary grids. The grid count
used here is the existing finite-grid complement-cardinality bound.

The source context is the upper-bound argument of Theorem 5.16 and the upper-bound consequence of
Proposition 5.17 and Theorem 5.18 in [BOOS24-v3](references.md#BOOS24-v3), together with Lemma
5.11's Hom and resolution comparison. The library reuses its actual two-injective rows and
minimal-resolution comparison, proves finite-dimensionality and quotient transport for the actual
Hom cokernel, and specializes the resulting bounds to finite coordinate grids. The real-coordinate
transfer is stated and documented separately.
