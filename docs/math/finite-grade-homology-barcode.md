# Canonical barcodes of critical-grade homology

For a filter function on a finite collection of faces, `gradeFiltration` samples the sublevel complexes at the distinct attained face values. At every sampled grade, `simplicialHomologyPModule` uses the existing simplicial chain complex and the inclusion-induced homology maps. The number of grades is `N = f.criticalValues.card`; there is no artificial grade when the complex is empty.

The reusable `simplicialHomology_finiteDimensional` instance needs only finiteness of the degree-`n` faces. Its vertex and coefficient field universes are independent. Degree-`n` chains are finite-dimensional Finsupps; their cycle submodule and boundary quotient are finite-dimensional, and the existing homology-to-quotient linear equivalence transfers this to homology. No finiteness of degree-`n+1` faces is needed. At a critical grade, face inclusion into the ambient finite face set supplies the required local finiteness.

`gradeHomologyBarcode k f n` applies the [finite rank barcode](finite-rank-barcode.md) to that homology persistence module. It requires vertex and field types in the same universe because the existing `rankBarcode` API requires its carrier universe to equal the field universe. The grade-value universe is independent. For `i ≤ j`, `rank_gradeHomologyMap` equates the rank of the inclusion-induced homology map to the count of intervals with birth at or before `i` and death after `j`. The equality follows from canonical rank recovery and the rank formula for a realized barcode; it is not inferred from the dimensions of the two endpoint spaces.

The examples use empty, singleton, and two-disconnected-vertex filters. The empty filter has zero grades and an empty barcode. A singleton has ordinary, unreduced one-dimensional `H₀`, zero `H₁`, and one `[0,1)` bar. For two vertices entering at separate grades, the `H₀` dimensions are one and two; the inclusion has rank one by tracking a nonzero vertex class through the quotient naturality map, and the barcode is `{[0,2), [1,2)}`. The singleton and two-vertex facts are checked over both `ℚ` and `ZMod 2`. Death `N` is the last observed grade sentinel, not infinity.

This module constructs a finite-index rank barcode of homology; it does not itself construct a value-labelled diagram or an executable reducer, or interpret essential death as infinity. The later [finite interval decomposition](finite-interval-decomposition.md) proves `nonempty_rankBarcodeIso` for every pointwise finite-dimensional `PModule.{u,0,u}` on `Fin N`, including this sampled homology module, giving a genuine natural isomorphism to the realization of its canonical barcode. The isomorphism is generally noncanonical and proof-only.
