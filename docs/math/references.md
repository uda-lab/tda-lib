# Source register and mathematical mappings

This is the canonical bibliography for existing library sources and the five next
[README directions](../../README.md#scope-and-limitations). Stable keys identify consulted
manifestations, not interchangeable editions of a work. Dates distinguish initial public
submission, version revision, online publication and issue year. Evidence was inspected
on 2026-10-14. Missing evidence is labeled unresolved; neither a citation nor an accessible
PDF establishes permission for every use. Errata and edition comparisons are limited to
the corrections and differences explicitly recorded below; absence of another correction
is not a certified exhaustive errata search. No current institutional affiliations or
Japanese-name spellings are inferred from publication-era romanized attribution.

The mathematical mappings below preserve the existing digests. **Source match** means a
selected source statement/bridge checked directly; **specialization** retains an explicit
restricted domain; **extension** is a library argument beyond the attributed statement;
**background** carries no implemented theorem claim. The bounded #215 reviews are recorded
in its PRs: PH includes direct selected source comparisons; Mapper includes direct DMW/CO
comparison; Reeb includes direct selected DMP comparison, with graph/PL/adaptation portions
reviewed from digests. They are not complete reviews of every paper or future milestone.

## Rights profiles and intended acts

The register contains metadata, links and original mathematical summaries. It does not
redistribute articles, figures, supplements or third-party code, except for the previously
identified software adaptation under its own license. Reading, limited quotation,
AI-assisted research input/processing, local copies and repository reproduction are
separate acts. Training and bulk collection are outside the requested use; no permission
for them is inferred. No jurisdictional copyright exception is relied on for clearance.

## Existing declaration and proof mappings

These mappings retain theorem numbers, conventions and explicit library extensions from
the mathematical digests. Paper citation keys resolve to the manifestation cards above;
software links resolve to immutable versions. A source reference is not a claim that its
entire article or all its algorithms have been formalized.

- [BOOS24-v3](#BOOS24-v3) — [Botnan–Oppermann–Oudot–Scoccola, *On the bottleneck stability of rank decompositions of multi-parameter persistence modules*, Proposition 5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.00300v3#S5.SS1): `TDA.Persistence.GeneratedUpsetResolution` constructs the actual join-indexed resolution and proves ordinary projectivity, chain laws, and exactness at every lattice index via the simplex on active generator occurrences. Repeated values retain their occurrence indices. Minimality and global-dimension results are not claimed; see [the proof exposition](generated-upset-resolution.md).

- [BOOS24-v3](#BOOS24-v3) — §2.4, Definition 5.4, Notation 5.9, and [Lemmas 5.6–5.7](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.00300v3#S5.SS1): `TDA.Persistence.IncidenceQuotientBaseChange` realizes the right-module quotient base-change construction for the actual interval-pair incidence algebra by factoring the action through its quotient. The library proves its quotient universal property, projective and finite-colimit preservation, and the canonical degree-zero vertex comparison. `TDA.Persistence.IncidenceQuotientDerivedOne` proves exactness in degree one after applying extension to any projective resolution of a restricted quotient module, and the resulting first left-derived vanishing. `TDA.Persistence.IncidenceQuotientColumnAction` realizes the higher-degree projective-ideal/column mechanism of Lemma 5.6 by an additive functor and a natural assembly with image the actual quotient relations and injective on projectives. `TDA.Persistence.IncidenceQuotientDerived` proves exactness at every degree `n ≥ 2` after extension of any projective resolution of any incidence module, with actual categorical left-derived vanishing there; for restricted quotient modules it proves exactness and vanishing in every positive degree. These statements leave the unaugmented degree-zero comparison unchanged and make no numerical dimension or `2n−2` persistence claim. See [right-module base change along the diagonal quotient](incidence-quotient-base-change.md). The minimal-complement count used in Theorem 5.16 is formalized separately: `TDA.Persistence.FiniteLatticeComplementCardinality` proves the product, adjoined-top, and actual interval-pair counts, while `TDA.Persistence.FiniteGridInjectiveRankDimension` derives the arbitrary-field ordinary-injective Hom bound from them. This is only the finite-grid counting layer, not the global-dimension or `2n−2` conclusion; see [minimal-complement cardinalities in finite grids](finite-grid-complement-cardinality.md).
